# AI-generated art (SI-generated)

All images here were made with Gemini (Nano Banana) from our prompts, then resized (and, where noted in content/sources.csv, lightly cropped or retouched).

## Honesty note (do not remove)
The scenes (Angola drought, ground station, Jeddah floods, Earth, spacecraft) are **illustrations, not real places, data or satellite imagery**. Do not present them as such in the app, slides, video or pitch. Real imagery comes from the **NASA GIBS slot on the Report screen**, with its NASA GIBS credit.

## SI tag rules
- In the app: every image is rendered with the `.sp-si-tag` ("SI-generated") visible on it. This folder holds the untagged files for that reason.
- In slides, video and any export: use the tagged copies in `content/pitch-art/*-si.webp` (the tag is burned in). Never use an untagged file outside the app.
- No NASA logos, flags or insignia; no people. Art-P7 (Jeddah floods) is stretch-only, after tag v1.0-rc.
